Offline location data (resources/locations.tsv)

Contains place names and coordinates derived from GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org/),
as packaged by reverse_geocode 1.6.6 (https://github.com/richardpenman/reverse_geocode).

GeoNames data is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
License (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The records were reduced to latitude, longitude, country, region and city columns.
